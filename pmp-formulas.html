Core Measurements

Planned Value (PV)EVM
PV = % Planned Complete × BAC

Work that should have been completed by a specific point in time.

BACBudget at Completion (total project budget)
Earned Value (EV)EVM
EV = % Actually Complete × BAC

The value of the work that has actually been accomplished.

Actual Cost (AC)EVM
AC = Actual cost incurred

Total cost actually spent to accomplish the work measured by EV.

Variances

Cost Variance (CV)Variance
CV = EV − AC

Positive = under budget. Negative = over budget.

Schedule Variance (SV)Variance
SV = EV − PV

Positive = ahead of schedule. Negative = behind schedule.

Performance Indices

Cost Performance Index (CPI)Index
CPI = EV ÷ AC

Efficiency of cost usage. CPI > 1 = under budget, < 1 = over budget.

⚑ Most important EVM efficiency metric on the exam.

Schedule Performance Index (SPI)Index
SPI = EV ÷ PV

Efficiency of schedule usage. SPI > 1 = ahead of schedule.

Forecasting

Estimate at Completion (EAC) — 3 variantsForecast
EAC = BAC ÷ CPI

If current CPI trend continues for the rest of the project.

EAC = AC + (BAC − EV)

If the remaining work will be done at the originally planned rate.

EAC = AC + [(BAC − EV) ÷ (CPI × SPI)]

If both cost and schedule performance affect remaining work.

Estimate to Complete (ETC)Forecast
ETC = EAC − AC

Cost remaining to finish the project from now.

Variance at Completion (VAC)Forecast
VAC = BAC − EAC

Expected cost over/under budget at project end.

To-Complete Performance Index (TCPI)Forecast
TCPI = (BAC − EV) ÷ (BAC − AC)

CPI efficiency required on remaining work to meet the BAC.

TCPI = (BAC − EV) ÷ (EAC − AC)

Use this version when the original BAC is no longer achievable.

Critical Path Method

Float (Slack)CPM
Float = LS − ES or LF − EF
LSLate Start
ESEarly Start
LFLate Finish
EFEarly Finish

⚑ Critical path activities have zero float.

Early Start / Early FinishCPM
EF = ES + Duration − 1

Forward pass: move left to right through the network. ES of successor = EF of predecessor + 1.

Late Start / Late FinishCPM
LS = LF − Duration + 1

Backward pass: move right to left. LF of predecessor = LS of successor − 1.

PERT (3-Point Estimating)

PERT Estimate (Beta)PERT
E = (O + 4M + P) ÷ 6
OOptimistic estimate
MMost Likely estimate
PPessimistic estimate
PERT Standard DeviationPERT
σ = (P − O) ÷ 6

Measures the spread/uncertainty of the estimate.

PERT VariancePERT
σ² = ((P − O) ÷ 6)²

Sum of activity variances along the critical path gives the project variance.

Triangular Distribution3-Point
E = (O + M + P) ÷ 3

Simpler than PERT beta — each estimate carries equal weight.

Communication

Communication ChannelsComms
Channels = n(n − 1) ÷ 2

Total potential communication paths between n stakeholders.

⚑ Very frequently tested. With 10 people → 10×9÷2 = 45 channels.

Procurement & Cost

Point of Total Assumption (PTA)Contract
PTA = ((Ceiling − Target Price) ÷ Buyer Share) + Target Cost

In a Fixed Price Incentive Fee (FPIF) contract — the cost at which the seller absorbs all additional cost risk.

Contract Price (CPIF / CPFF)Contract
Price = AC + Fee

Cost-Plus contracts: buyer pays all actual costs plus an agreed fee (fixed, incentive, or award).

Depreciation

Straight-Line DepreciationFinance
Depreciation = (Asset Cost − Salvage Value) ÷ Useful Life

Equal depreciation expense each year.

Double Declining BalanceFinance
Depreciation = (2 ÷ Useful Life) × Book Value

Accelerated method — higher depreciation in early years.

Project Selection

Net Present Value (NPV)Selection
NPV = Σ [FV ÷ (1 + r)ⁿ] − Initial Investment
FVFuture cash flow
rDiscount rate
nPeriod number

⚑ Higher NPV = better project. NPV > 0 means the project adds value.

Present Value (PV)Finance
PV = FV ÷ (1 + r)ⁿ

What a future amount is worth in today's dollars.

Return on Investment (ROI)Selection
ROI = (Net Benefits ÷ Costs) × 100%

Higher ROI = more attractive project. Used for benefit-cost comparisons.

Risk

Expected Monetary Value (EMV)Risk
EMV = Probability × Impact

Used in decision tree analysis. Sum of all EMVs = Expected Value of a decision branch.

⚑ Risks use negative values; opportunities use positive values.

Contingency ReserveRisk
Reserve = Σ (Probability × Impact) per risk

Set aside for identified risks ("known unknowns"). Management reserve handles "unknown unknowns."

Quality

Cost of Quality (COQ)Quality
COQ = Cost of Conformance + Cost of Non-Conformance

Cost of conformance = prevention + appraisal costs. Cost of non-conformance = internal + external failure costs.

Control Chart — Rule of SevenQuality
Alert: 7 consecutive points on same side of mean

Seven consecutive data points above or below the mean indicates a non-random pattern — the process is out of control even if within control limits.

Team & Human Resources

Tuckman's Team DevelopmentHR
Forming → Storming → Norming → Performing → Adjourning

Not a numerical formula, but a required model: teams progress through stages, and conflict (storming) is a normal part of development.

Maslow's Hierarchy of NeedsHR
Physiological → Safety → Social → Esteem → Self-Actualization

Lower-level needs must be met before higher-level motivators take effect.
